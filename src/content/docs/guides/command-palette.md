The command palette is a **note search** tool: it finds open notes (tabs) by their name or their content, so you can jump to any note in a couple of keystrokes.

## Open the palette

- Press `Ctrl+K` anywhere (even while typing in the editor).
- Click the **Search** button at the top of the left sidebar.
- On mobile, use the **Search** button in the bottom bar.

## Recent notes

With an empty search box, the palette shows **Recent**: the up to three most recently edited notes.

## Searching

- The search matches both the **note title** and the **note content**.
- Matching is case-insensitive and substring-based, so results are found wherever the query appears.
- Results carry a badge: **Title** when the name matched, **Content** when the body did.
- Matching text is highlighted, and content matches show a short snippet with `…`.

## Keyboard navigation

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `↑` / `↓` | Move through results (wraps around) |
| `Enter` | Open the selected note |
| `Esc` | Close the palette |

You can also click a result to open it, or click outside to close.
