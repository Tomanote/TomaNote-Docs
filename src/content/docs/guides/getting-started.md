TomaNote is a minimal, offline-first note-taking web app.

## Create a note

- Open TomaNote and start typing in the editor to create a new note.
- Your notes are saved automatically to your browser's local storage.

## Organize your notes

- Use tabs to manage multiple notes at once.
- Pin your favorite tabs to keep them at hand.
