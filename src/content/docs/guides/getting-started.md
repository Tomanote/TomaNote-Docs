TomaNote is a minimalist, offline-first note-taking web app. It runs entirely in your browser: there are no accounts, no servers and nothing to install. Your notes are saved automatically on your own device.

## Open TomaNote

Visit [tomanote.app](https://tomanote.app). You can start writing right away, without signing up.

## Create your first note

- Start typing in the editor to create a new note.
- Use the **+** button in the right sidebar (desktop) or the bottom bar (mobile), or press `Alt+N`, to open another note in a new tab.
- Your notes stay open as tabs, just like a browser.

## Autosave

- Every note is saved **automatically** as you type.
- A **Saved** indicator appears at the bottom-right of the editor when you pause typing (about five seconds).
- Press `Ctrl+S` at any time to see instant save feedback.

## Organize your notes

- Use **tabs** to manage multiple notes at once.
- **Pin** your favorite tabs to keep them at hand (`Alt+P`).
- Search all your notes with the command palette (`Ctrl+K`).

## Install as an app (PWA)

TomaNote is an installable progressive web app:

- In the browser address bar, click the **install** icon (Chrome/Edge) or use the browser menu.
- On mobile, use **Add to Home screen**.
- Once installed, TomaNote opens in its own window and works **offline**, thanks to a service worker.
