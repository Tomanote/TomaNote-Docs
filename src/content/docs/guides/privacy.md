title: Privacy & data
description: Where your notes are stored and how to keep them safe.

TomaNote is **offline-first and private by design**: your notes never leave your device.

## Where your notes live

- Notes and tabs are stored in your browser's **local storage** (under the key `tabsData`).
- There are **no accounts, no servers and no external data transmission**.
- The app works offline, and an installed version (PWA) caches the app itself for offline use.

## Limits to know

- Browsers cap local storage at about **5 MB** per origin. Very long notes, or many notes, could approach this limit.
- **Clearing your browser's site data (cache / storage) deletes your notes.** There is no built-in export/import yet, so if you plan to clear data, copy important notes elsewhere first.

## Keeping your notes safe

- Don't rely on TomaNote as the only copy of critical information.
- Keep a backup by copying note text to a file while the app is running.
- Avoid storing highly sensitive information — data lives locally, but a shared device is a shared risk.
