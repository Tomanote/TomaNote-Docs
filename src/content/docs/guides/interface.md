title: Interface overview

TomaNote's interface is split into a few clearly separated regions. On larger screens you get a left sidebar, a horizontal tab strip, the editor and a right sidebar with tools. On smaller screens the toolbars move to a bottom bar.

![interface-desktop-light](/screenshots/interface-desktop-light.png)

## Left sidebar (desktop)

The narrow left sidebar gives you quick access to the app's main actions, each with a tooltip:

- **Search** — opens the command palette (`Ctrl+K`).
- **Keyboard shortcuts** — opens the shortcuts help (`Alt+/`).
- **Settings** — opens the settings modal (`Alt+S`).

The TomaNote logo sits at the top of the sidebar.

![interface-sidebar-left](/screenshots/interface-sidebar-left.png)

## Tab strip

Your open notes live as tabs in a horizontal strip at the top of the main area:

- Click a tab to switch to that note.
- Hover a tab to reveal its **rename** and **delete** buttons.
- **Pinned** tabs stay at the front and keep their emoji.
- Drag a tab to reorder it, or press `Alt+1` to `Alt+9` to jump to a specific tab.
- Press **+** (or `Alt+N`) to create a new note.

## Editor

The main area is the editor, where you write. Its paper background can be **flat**, **underlined** or **gridded** (see [Settings](/guides/settings/)). When there are no open notes, an empty state appears: "Oops… nothing here!".

![interface-empty-state](/screenshots/interface-empty-state.png)

## Right sidebar (desktop)

On desktop (screens of 768 px and wider) a static panel on the right side hosts the tools:

- **+** — create a new note.
- **Content** — redo, undo, copy, paste.
- **Font** — bold, underline, italic.
- **Tab** — pin, rename, delete the active tab.

See [Tools & right sidebar](/guides/tools/) for details.

## Bottom bar (mobile)

On smaller screens the toolbars collapse into a bottom bar with the same **Content**, **Font** and **Tab** groups, plus **search**, **settings** and a **new note** button.

![interface-mobile](/screenshots/interface-mobile.png)

## Save indicator

When you pause typing, a small **Saved** indicator appears at the bottom-right of the editor. `Ctrl+S` shows it instantly.
